Cue-Preserving Binaural Speech Enhancement with Dual-Path and Polar-Decoupling Network

Aleksej Chinaev, Gerald Enzner

In the following we provide audio samples of 15 neural networks that are involved in our evaluation: twelve DNNs for CoGaPha-related ablation study and three external baselines such as BinMap(FCRN), BinConvTasNet and CoGa(CDAE). The audio samples are available for two datasets "testKiekko" and "testWham" generated for various conditions of ambient noise. For each dataset, we provide audio samples for different acoustic environments, including a bathroom shower, a church visit, a restaurant, and an office building. Every audio sample was generated for individual signal-to-noise ratio (SNR), direction of arrival (DOA) and noise type. The CoGaPha-based ablation models are configured with:
  • two system setups, i.e, bilateral (Bil) vs. binaural (Bin)
  • three regression styles, i.e., mapping (Map) and real-valued spectral gain (Ga) regression vs. the gain-phase (GaPha) regression in our proposed CoGaPha system
  • two ''inner'' architectures,i.e, complex convolutional recurrent (cCRN) vs.\ the proposed dual-path time-frequency cCRN (DPcCRN) model.
